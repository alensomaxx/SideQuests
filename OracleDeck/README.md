# 🃏 The Oracle Deck

A mesmerizing **card prediction trick** built with **Python** and **PyQt6**, designed to astound your audience by revealing their chosen card with uncanny accuracy. This project was a deep dive into **GUI development**, **custom widget creation**, and implementing **classic algorithmic magic tricks** in a digital format.

## ✨ Features

- 🔮 **Mystical Card Prediction**
  Experience the classic 21-card trick, reimagined digitally.

- 🖼️ **Elegant & Responsive UI**
  Built with PyQt6, featuring a modern, clean design that adapts to various window sizes.

- 🎨 **Custom Theming with QSS**
  Styled with Nord theme colors for a visually pleasing and consistent aesthetic across all screens.

- 🌟 **Interactive Card Display**
  Hover effects and clear presentation for easy card selection and pile identification.

- 🪄 **Dynamic Pile Management**
  Cards are automatically dealt and reassembled into piles based on user input, guiding the magic.

- 🎛️ **Intuitive Controls**
  Simple radio button selections and clear navigation buttons make the trick easy to follow.

## 🚀 How to Play

1.  **Start the Game**: Click "Start Game" on the welcome screen.
2.  **Choose Your Card**: Mentally select any one of the 21 cards displayed. **Do not tell the computer your choice!**
3.  **Identify the Pile**: The cards will be dealt into three piles. Select the pile that contains your chosen card.
4.  **Repeat**: This process will repeat for a total of three rounds.
5.  **The Reveal**: After three rounds, the Oracle Deck will magically reveal your chosen card!
